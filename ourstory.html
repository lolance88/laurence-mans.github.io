October 2024

📱 The Hinge Match

Like many modern love stories, ours began with a swipe.

Laurence’s opening gambit wasn’t charm, wit, or good looks — it was the bold claim that he could cook Malaysian food.

Against all logic and reason, this worked.

🇻🇳 The Vietnam Problem

Their first date was a whirlwind of efficiency and unexpected chemistry. It went so well that they accidently planned their entire future together. You know, completely normal first-date conversations.

Despite the fantastic first date, there was one issue. Laurence was due to travel to Vietnam for a month.

👯‍♀️ Thank You, Mystery Friend

Manstanue, being the sensible one, assumed the spark would fizzle out due to distance, time zones, and the fact that he was about to spend four weeks travelling around Vietnam with his friends.

She was prepared to call it there, and had it not been for one very persuasive friend convincing Manstanue, this story might have ended after date one.

Fortunately for Laurence - and unfortunately for that friend who now has to live with the consequences - she agreed to date number two the day before he left for Vietnam.

📞 Operation: Prove Her Wrong

Laurence promised the distance wouldn't matter. And somehow, despite being on the other side of the world, he actually delivered.

They spoke every single day while he was in Vietnam.

By the end of the trip, it was clear this wasn't just a holiday romance optimism.

🛬 Hanoi to Heathrow to Leyton

Upon landing back in London, Laurence didn't even pretend to be relaxed about the situation.

He went straight from Heathrow Airport to Manstanue's flat.

No detours. No luggage (the airline lost it). No recovery nap.

Just a direct airport-to-girlfriend service.

December 2024

🇲🇾 The Plot Twist Nobody Saw Coming

After only a few weeks together, another obstacle appeared. Manstanue was due to return to Malaysia for a month to visit her family she hadn't seen in over two years.

Normally this would have meant another long-distance chapter. However, Laurence had recently made a slightly unhinged life decision.

Whilst sitting on a boat in Ha Long Bay at the dead of night, he called his CEO and quit his job.

As one does...

🫶 The Leap of Faith

With no job tying him down and a growing suspicion that he'd found something special, Laurence did the obvious thing. He flew to Malaysia.

What started as a promising relationship quickly became an unforgettable adventure together as they soon came to realise their families lived 5 minutes away from one another!

Laurence spent a couple weeks in Malaysia, navigating his next career move and meeting Manstanue's family and friends, as they connected through the culture and cuisine of their homeland.

March 2025

🏡 The Biggest Leap Yet

Fresh from Malaysia and full of confidence, they did what any sensible couple who'd only been together a short time would do...

They moved in together.

Neither of them had lived with a partner before, which meant there was a lot to learn.

Who knew there were so many ways to load the dishwasher incorrectly? Or that one person could own so many shoes?

Like any couple, they had their ups and downs. Though slowly but surely, their flat became their home.

December 2025

🇲🇾 Return to Malaysia

It was time for Manstanue to return to Malaysia for her annual visit to her family.

On the surface, Laurence was joining to spend more time with Manstanue's family. Whilst that remained true, he had another reason...

He had a very important question to ask - and it wasn't for Manstanue.

😰 The Interview

Before asking Manstanue for her hand in marriage, Laurence needed permission from the people who mattered most - Mr & Mrs Tan.

There were questions. There were follow-up questions. There may have even been trick questions.

After a thorough review process, and by some miracle, Laurence somehow passed and received the Tan blessing.

May 2026

🇯🇵 Trip to Japan

With the blessing of Mr & Mrs Tan secured, Laurence was ready to propose.

He had a plan. He had the ring. The vision was perfect.

A romantic setting. A stunning view of Mt Fuji. A beautiful hotel. An unforgettable moment.

What could possibly go wrong?

⛰️ Fuji had other plans...

As it turns out, quite a lot.

The day the proposal arrived, the weather did too.

Clouds rolled in. Rain followed. Mt Fuji, the star attraction and centrepiece of the entire plan, was nowhere to be seen.

Despite staying at an expensive hotel specifically chosen for the view, Fuji decided it would not be making an appearance.

💍 The Proposal

At this point Laurence had two options: 1) Wait indefinitely for better weather, or 2) Accept that perfection isn't what makes a moment special.

With no mountain backdrop, no postcard perfect scenery, and a proposal plan that had been thoroughly sabotaged by Mother Nature, he got down on one knee and asked the question he’d travelled halfway around the world to ask.

Despite the missing mountain... Despite the rain... Despite everything not going according to plan...

She said yes.

August 2026

❤️ Our Greatest Adventure Yet

Looking back, our story has never really gone to accordingly to plan.

A first date before a month apart...

A spontaneous trip to Malaysia...

Moving in together sooner than expected...

A proposal without the mountain that was supposed to be in the background...

But somehow, the best parts of our story have happened when we’ve taken a leap of faith and embraced the unexpected.

And now we can’t wait to begin our next chapter together — with all of you by our side.

Laurence & Manstanue · 28th August 2026
